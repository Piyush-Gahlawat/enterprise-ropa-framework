# Data Privacy Methodology: GDPR & DPDP Act

*This document outlines the methodology for maintaining a Record of Processing Activities (ROPA) under GDPR Article 30 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 (India).*

## 1. What is a ROPA?
A ROPA is a mandatory register that documents every activity where an organization processes personal data. If an auditor or regulator asks, "What data do you have, where is it, and why?", the ROPA is the answer.

## 2. GDPR vs. DPDP (The Key Differences)
| Feature | GDPR (Europe) | DPDP Act (India) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Basis** | Requires a specific basis (Consent, Contract, Legitimate Interest, etc.) | Primarily based on **Consent**, with limited "Legitimate Uses". |
| **DSAR Deadline** | 30 Days | "Reasonable time" (practically 30 days). |
| **Data Principal Rights** | 8 rights (Access, Erasure, Portability, etc.) | 4 rights (Access, Correction, Erasure, Grievance Redressal). |
| **Consent Managers** | Not a distinct legal entity concept. | Explicitly requires registered "Consent Managers". |

## 3. The ROPA Lifecycle
1. **Data Mapping:** Interviewing business units (HR, Sales, IT) to find where data lives.
2. **Classification:** Categorizing data (e.g., PII, Sensitive PII).
3. **Legal Basis Assignment:** Why are we allowed to process this?
4. **Retention Definition:** How long do we keep it before deleting it?
5. **Review:** Auditing the ROPA every 6 months.
